## Brand & Style

The design system translates campus peer-to-peer barter into a sleek, nocturnal, high-contrast digital trading ground. Tailored for student communities trading books, dorm gear, high-end electronics, and creative work, the visual narrative shifts away from daylight minimalism into an immersive, neon-accented cyber-campus space.

### Brand Character
- **Nocturnal & Focused:** Deep slate foundations eliminate late-night eye strain while spotlighting colorful item photography and active trade exchanges.
- **Trustworthy & Validated:** Luminous emerald verification markers, verified `.edu` badges, and precise divider rules create an undeniable aura of security against campus scams.
- **Energetic & Frictionless:** Vivid barter orange highlights signal active offers, wanted items, and instant trades without visual chaos.
- **Design Style:** Modern High-Contrast Dark Minimal with tactile glass accents—grounded on dark obsidian slabs, razor-sharp slate borders, crisp typography, and glowing semantic beacons.

## Colors

The dark color system is engineered for maximum legibility, high visual separation between "Have" and "Want" trading sides, and vibrant accessibility against dark surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#090d16` / `#0f172a`):** The deep obsidian underlayer establishing a dark, distraction-free environment.
- **Surface Card (`#1e293b`):** The primary container tier for trade cards, floating controls, and navigation bars.
- **Elevated Surface (`#1f2937`):** Raised popovers, contextual drawers, and modal sheets.
- **Border Dividers (`#334155`):** Structural boundaries that keep dark elements neatly framed without relying on aggressive fills. Hover interactions elevate borders to `#475569`.

### Functional Accents
- **Primary Emerald Teal (`#14b8a6`, `#2dd4bf`):** Applied to core trade CTAs, active tab indicators, student verification shields, and "HAVE / VERİLEN" tags.
- **Secondary Barter Orange (`#f97316`, `#fb923c`):** Highlights urgent offers, dynamic counters, notification dots, and the "WANT / ARANAN" trade side.
- **Text Layers:** Primary titles and key metrics leverage `#f8fafc` for maximum luminescence. Secondary details use `#e2e8f0`, while metadata, timestamps, and placeholders utilize `#94a3b8`.

## Typography

Typography relies completely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity and open apertures to prevent dense dark-mode halation or visual smearing.

- **Contrast Allocation:** Display titles (`#f8fafc`) maintain crisp authority against `#0f172a`. Explanatory paragraphs, trade specifications, and rules adopt `#e2e8f0` to lower optical glare during sustained reading.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Captions, campus distances, and status tokens use medium and semibold weights (`600` / `700`) with generous letter spacing to stay legible on elevated dark surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column grid structures content across wide displays, scaling gracefully down to 6 columns on tablets and a focused single-column or 2-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Canvas Limits:** Max layout container extends to `1280px` with responsive margin padding (`margin-mobile` at 16px to `margin` at 32px on desktop).
- **Feed Rhythm:** Barter item grids configure via CSS auto-fill columns (`minmax(300px, 1fr)`) separated by `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Component Padding:** Inner card contents, dialogue modals, and exchange drawers leverage `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), granting generous breathing room between dense product listings and user exchange inputs.

## Elevation & Depth

In a high-contrast dark environment, drop shadows are virtually imperceptible unless heavily boosted or tinted. The system uses a combination of **tonal surface escalation, hairline borders, and neon ambient glows**:

- **Ground Level 0 (Backdrop):** `#090d16` canvas background with zero elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Listing Cards, Navbars):** Background `#1e293b` outlined with a crisp `1px solid #334155` border. Casts a soft ambient occlusion shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns):** Background `#1f2937` with border shifted to `#475569`. Emits a teal-tinted ambient glow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Overlays):** Background `#1f2937` supported by a frosted scrim (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(9, 13, 22, 0.75)`). Framed by `1px solid #475569` and deep ambient shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced Level 2 (`Rounded`) aesthetic:

- **Core Geometry:** Cards and modal dialogs use `1rem` (16px) corners, keeping large panels soft yet structured.
- **Interactive Controls:** Form fields, standard action buttons, and product image previews leverage `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Badges & Campus Filters:** Filter tags, verified student badges, and counter pills take complete pill rounding (`9999px`) for intuitive finger-friendly tactile tapping.

## Components

### 1. Barter Listing Card ('Have' vs 'Want')
The signature component of the platform re-engineered for dark mode:
- **Frame:** `#1e293b` container enclosed by a clean `1px solid #334155` border, rounded to `1rem`.
- **The 'Have' Zone (Sahip Olunan):** Deep teal-tinted background (`#0c2826`) on image header badge. Tagged with an emerald pill `VERİLEN` (`#14b8a6` background with `#090d16` dark text for high-contrast punch). Primary item title displayed in crisp `#f8fafc`.
- **The Divider:** Razor-thin divider (`#334155`) intersected by a circular exchange icon badge (`#1f2937` with `#2dd4bf` icon).
- **The 'Want' Zone (Aranan):** Deep amber-tinted background tier (`#2a1b14`) outlined with a subtle `#f97316` (20% opacity) border. Badged with `ARANAN` (`#f97316` fill, `#090d16` dark text). Description rendered in high-contrast `#e2e8f0`.
- **Footer:** Micro avatar with glowing online dot, verified campus shield (`#2dd4bf`), campus building distance tag (`#94a3b8`), and the primary swap trigger button.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Swap Button:** Vibrant emerald fill (`#14b8a6`) with dark slate text (`#090d16`, font weight 700). On hover: `#2dd4bf` with a subtle teal bloom shadow.
- **Make Offer Accent Button:** Bright barter orange fill (`#f97316`) with dark slate text (`#090d16`, font weight 700). On hover: `#fb923c` with a soft orange bloom.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Translucent fill (`transparent`), crisp border (`#334155`), and light text (`#e2e8f0`). On hover: background `#1f2937` and border turns `#14b8a6`.

### 3. Filter & Category Pills
- Pill contour (`9999px`), padding `0.375rem 1rem`.
- **Inactive:** `#1e293b` fill, `#334155` border, `#94a3b8` text.
- **Active:** Glowing emerald `#14b8a6` fill, dark text (`#090d16`), paired with a checkmark icon.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#0f172a` encased in `1px solid #334155` with rounded `0.5rem`.
- Text typed appears in `#f8fafc`; placeholder set to `#94a3b8`.
- Focus state casts a dual ring: `border-color: #14b8a6` and an outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.25)`.

### 5. Campus Trust & Karma Badges
- **Verified Student:** Pill badge with `#0c2826` fill, `#14b8a6` hairline border, and `#2dd4bf` text with a verification tick.
- **Trade Karma Counter:** Deep card pill with glowing swap icon and bold `#fb923c` numeric trade score.
