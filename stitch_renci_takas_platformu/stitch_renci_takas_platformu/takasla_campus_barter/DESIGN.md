## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a fusion of modern Scandinavian functional minimalism and warm, spirited campus vibrancy. Built for university peers trading textbooks, dorm essentials, electronics, and clothing, the UI balances trust and safety with an approachable, social energy. 

Key attributes:
- **Circular & Sustainable:** Reinforces reuse and responsible zero-waste student living without clinical sterility.
- **Trustworthy & Safe:** High-clarity typography, deliberate verification markers, and transparent peer credentials create a secure, scam-resistant campus environment.
- **Energetic & Playful:** Micro-interactions and warm accent pops evoke campus club culture, bulletin board exchanges, and friction-free social swaps.
- **Design Movement:** Nordic Modern with tactile, humanistic accents—spacious layouts, structured grids, soft geometric contours, and selective energetic highlights.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of fresh environmental reuse tempered by human warmth:

- **Primary (`#0D9488` - Deep Emerald Teal):** Signifies circular economy, ecological consciousness, and operational stability. Used for primary calls-to-action, navigation anchors, active state indicators, and confirmed swap milestones.
- **Secondary (`#F97316` - Warm Amber Coral):** Injects spontaneous energy, conversation triggers, notification indicators, swap bid highlights, and the "Looking For / Want" counter-indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Mint Emerald):** Applied to success confirmations, student university verification badges, online presence indicators, and item condition ratings.
- **Neutral Tone (`#0F172A` - Deep Slate):** Anchors headings and high-priority content, avoiding pure black for softer reading strain while preserving WCAG AAA contrast ratios against paper-white and canvas tiers.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Cool Crisp Off-White).
  - Surface Card: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  - Subtle Fill / Disabled / Muted: `#F1F5F9`.
  - Border Lines: `#E2E8F0` for structural containers, tinted to `#CBD5E1` on interactive hover.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric build coupled with humanist curves delivers clean readability for item specifications while feeling friendly, contemporary, and approachable.

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (-0.01em to -0.03em) generate editorial presence without feeling aggressive. Hero and section titles speak boldly to the student demographic.
- **Card Titles & Subheaders:** Weighted at Semibold (600) to maintain visual priority even over photographic item thumbnails.
- **Body & Captions:** Ample line heights preserve legibility on density-heavy swap cards, campus locations, and trading rules.
- **Badges & Labels:** Small caps and uppercase are reserved only for status tokens (e.g., `OFFER PENDING`, `VERIFIED STUDENT`).

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column grid layout controls content on screens 1024px and wider, transitioning into a 6-column grid for tablets (640px to 1023px) and a single or 2-column card stack for mobile devices (<640px).

- **Global Page Framing:** Outer page margins ensure breathable separation from screen bezels (`margin-mobile` at 16px, scaling to `margin` at 32px on desktop). Max desktop content container is fixed to `1280px`.
- **Card Grids:** Barter feeds arrange cards via CSS grid with dynamic auto-fill sizing (`minmax(280px, 1fr)`), maintaining an even `1.5rem` gutter.
- **Density Rules:** Compact micro-spacers (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) define relationships inside chip groups, metadata rows, and badge pairings. Component shells, cards, and modal dialogs rely on `space-md` to `space-lg` to retain clean separation.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with clean Scandinavian product styling, visual depth is achieved via soft tonal surfaces, subtle hairline borders, and ambient colored diffusion rather than muddy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base page background (`#F8FAFC`) with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Feed Tiles, Filter Bars):** Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a crisp `1px` border (`#E2E8F0`). Enhanced with a calm ambient floor shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Popovers):** Elevated card hover states and search dropdowns. Shadow expands to `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a primary teal-tinted ambient glow.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Contact Sheets):** Floating direct-contact cards and filter modals. Casts an extra-diffused shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over a semi-opaque neutral scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a Level 2 (`Rounded`) shape language, balancing friendly geometric curvature with structural efficiency:

- **Base Components:** Inputs, regular buttons, standard badging, and thumbnail previews use standard `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- **Cards & Modal Sheets:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to frame multi-tiered information distinctly.
- **Avatars, Status Tags & Campus Filter Pills:** Rely on full pill contours (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness and informal community exploration.

## Components

### 1. Barter Listing Card ('Have' vs 'Want')
The centerpiece component of the platform.
- **Structure:** Two-segment card enclosed in `rounded-lg` with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Top / Left (The "Have" - Sahip Olunan):** Contains photographic carousel with an emerald badge label `VERİLEN` (`#0D9488` background, white text). Displays condition tag, category tag, and item title.
- **Divider:** A playful transfer arrow indicator centered on the separator line.
- **Bottom / Right (The "Want" - Karşılığında Aranan):** Contrasting soft amber tint background (`#FFF7ED`) framed with a subtle `#FED7AA` line. Labeled with an orange tag `ARANAN` (`#F97316` background, white text). Lists specific swap target (e.g., "Kalkülüs 2 Kitabı ya da Filtre Kahve Makinesi").
- **Footer:** User avatar, university verification shield, distance indicator (e.g., "Kuzey Kampüs"), and primary contact trigger.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Swap Action:** Emerald teal background (`#0D9488`), white text, bold weight, `0.5rem` radius, subtle lift on hover with teal glow.
- **Secondary Interaction:** Crisp white background, slate border (`#E2E8F0`), slate text. On hover, background shifts to `#F8FAFC` and border adopts `#0D9488`.
- **Accent Offer Button:** Coral fill (`#F97316`), white text, reserved for "Teklif Yap" (Make Offer).

### 3. Filter & Category Pills
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.375rem 1rem`.
- Inactive: `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, zero border.
- Active: `#0D9488` background with `#FFFFFF` text and accompanying check icon.
- Specialized "Campus Selector" pill: Contains university pin icon and distinct badge showing live campus items count.

### 4. Direct Contact Action Popups & Sheets
- Triggered by clicking "İletişime Geç" on a listing card.
- Opens a concise modal sheet showing:
  - **Instagram DM Action:** Gradient-accented button leading to the student's verified profile handle.
  - **Campus Email Action:** One-click copy tool for verified `.edu.tr` address with a verified student trust marker.
  - Safe-exchange reminder: Brief prompt reminding users to meet in high-traffic campus spots (e.g., student union, library cafeteria).

### 5. Trust & Verification Badges
- **Verified Student:** Micro-badge with a green tick inside an emerald leaf/shield outline, containing the label `Doğrulanmış Öğrenci`.
- **Campus Karma Score:** Compact numeric rating badge paired with a swap hand icon indicating completed peer trades.

### 6. Inputs & Search Fields
- Rounded input fields (`0.5rem` radius) with `#F8FAFC` fills and high-contrast placeholder copy. Focus ring illuminates with an emerald teal outline (`#0D9488`, 2px offset).
- Campus dropdown selector integrated within the master search bar.
